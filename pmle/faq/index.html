What does the Professional Machine Learning Engineer certification cover?

The PMLE certification covers designing, building, productionizing, and improving machine learning and AI solutions on Google Cloud, including data preparation, model development, serving, MLOps, monitoring, and responsible AI.

How many PMLE practice questions are included?

Quiz Trail includes 400+ single-choice and multiple-choice PMLE practice questions with explanations and references. You can also read ten PMLE sample questions without signing in.

How do I start PMLE practice?

Open PMLE practice to try ten interactive questions as a guest. Sign in to open the complete bank and resume your saved PMLE progress.

How should I use the PMLE questions and summary?

Practice in short sessions and read the explanation after each answer. Bookmark questions to revisit and use your PMLE summary to find exam sections and objectives that need more work. Combine question practice with hands-on Google Cloud experience.

Where can I find current exam requirements?

Use Google’s official PMLE certification page and exam guide for current topics, registration details, and requirements.
