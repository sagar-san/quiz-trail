FAQ

Using Quiz Trail

Answers about signing in, saving your progress, and using Quiz Trail across certifications.

FAQ PMLE FAQ PAA FAQ

Is Quiz Trail free?

Yes. Quiz Trail is free and open source. Optional contributions through Buy Me a Coffee help cover cloud costs. Starring the project on GitHub is another way to support it.

Do I need an account?

You can try ten PMLE questions as a guest. Sign in with Google for the complete PMLE bank and saved progress across devices. Sign in on the homepage, then choose your certification. PAA practice is currently limited to the authorized preview account.

When is my progress saved?

When you are signed in, submitting an answer saves its latest result, and Save for later saves a bookmark. Guest answers and bookmarks last only for the current session and clear on reload or sign-in. Signing in loads your account’s saved progress; guest answers are not copied into it.

Can I use the same account for both certifications?

Yes. PMLE and PAA share one Google sign-in, with separate answers, bookmarks, filters, and summaries. Switching certifications keeps each set of progress separate. Access to a private preview still requires authorization.

What does the progress summary show?

Your summary shows coverage and latest-answer accuracy by exam section, objective, topic, and difficulty, plus queues for questions to revisit. It describes your practice activity, not a prediction of your exam result. Open the summary for the certification you are studying.

What data is stored, and can I delete it?

Quiz Trail uses basic Google account details to identify your account and stores your latest answer results and bookmarks. Google and Firebase provide authentication and cloud storage; Quiz Trail does not manage your Google password or sell your data. In Settings, you can reset the selected certification’s progress or delete your Quiz Trail account and progress across all certifications. This does not delete your Google account. Account deletion also removes your comment text, author names, and reactions. Older private feedback reports are retained separately and remain private.

How can I report a problem with a question?

After answering, open Discussion to post a comment visible to other signed-in learners. You can edit or delete your comments and Like or Dislike comments. Older private feedback reports remain private. You can also open a GitHub issue with the question ID and a description of the problem. Do not include personal or confidential exam information.

Are these real certification exam questions?

No. The bank contains original and curated practice material for learning. It does not provide exam dumps or claim that its questions appear on the certification exam.
