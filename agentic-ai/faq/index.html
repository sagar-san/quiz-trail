What does the Professional Agentic Architect certification cover?

Google Cloud’s Professional Agentic Architect certification covers designing, building, evaluating, deploying, and governing agentic AI systems. It includes low-code agents, custom agents, and coding-agent workflows.

Can I practice PAA questions on Quiz Trail yet?

Yes. Sign in with Google to practice all 150 PAA questions, save progress, and join discussions. Ten static PAA sample questions are also available without an account.

How do I open PAA practice and my summary?

Sign in on the homepage with your Google account, then choose Practice Agentic AI. The PAA summary shows only your PAA activity, with coverage, latest-answer accuracy, and review queues grouped by PAA exam sections, objectives, topics, and difficulty.

How should I prepare for the certification?

Use Google’s official PAA certification page and exam guide, training, and hands-on work with agentic systems. Question practice supports conceptual review; practical experience and any labs required by Google are also part of preparation.
