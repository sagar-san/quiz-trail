What does the Professional Agentic Architect certification cover?

Google Cloud’s Professional Agentic Architect certification covers designing, building, evaluating, deploying, and governing agentic AI systems. It includes low-code agents, custom agents, and coding-agent workflows.

Can I practice PAA questions on Quiz Trail yet?

Public PAA practice is coming soon. The question bank is being reviewed in a private preview. Only the authorized preview owner can open the practice questions or PAA summary; signing in with another account does not grant access.

How do I open the PAA preview and summary?

Sign in on the homepage with the authorized account, then choose Practice PAA preview. The PAA summary shows only your PAA activity, with coverage, latest-answer accuracy, and review queues grouped by PAA exam sections, objectives, topics, and difficulty.

How should I prepare while practice is coming soon?

Use Google’s official PAA certification page and exam guide, training, and hands-on work with agentic systems. Question practice supports conceptual review; practical experience and any labs required by Google are also part of preparation.

Already in the private preview?

Sign in with the authorized account to open PAA practice. Public access is coming soon.
