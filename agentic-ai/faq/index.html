PAA FAQ · Practice coming soon

Professional Agentic Architect practice, explained.

What to expect from PAA practice on Quiz Trail, how the private preview works, and where your progress lives.

What is the Professional Agentic Architect certification?

Google Cloud’s Professional Agentic Architect certification covers designing, building, evaluating, deploying, and governing agentic AI systems. It includes low-code agents, custom agents, and coding-agent workflows. Check the official certification page and exam guide for current requirements.

Can I practice PAA questions on Quiz Trail yet?

Public PAA practice is coming soon. The question bank is being reviewed in a private preview. Only the authorized preview owner can open the practice questions or PAA summary; signing in with another account does not grant access.

Is this an official Google Cloud question bank?

No. Quiz Trail is an independent study aid, not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. The practice material is for learning and does not claim to contain real certification exam questions.

How does the PAA summary work?

The PAA summary shows the preview owner’s PAA coverage, latest-answer accuracy, and review queues, grouped by exam section, objective, topic, and difficulty. It uses PAA activity only and is subject to the same private-preview access requirement as practice.

Will PAA affect my PMLE progress?

No. Both certifications use the same sign-in, but each has separate answers, bookmarks, filters, and summaries. Your existing PMLE progress stays in place. Resetting progress affects only the selected certification; deleting your Quiz Trail account removes progress for all certifications.

How should I prepare while practice is coming soon?

Use Google’s current exam guide, official training, and hands-on work with agentic systems. Quiz Trail’s question practice can support conceptual review, but it does not replace practical experience or any labs required by Google.

What data is saved during the private preview?

For the signed-in preview owner, submitting an answer saves that question’s latest result, and Save for later stores a bookmark. Google and Firebase provide account authentication and cloud storage. Settings includes progress reset and account deletion. Voluntary question feedback is stored separately for review.

Already in the private preview?

Sign in with the authorized account to practice or review your PAA progress. Public access is coming soon.
